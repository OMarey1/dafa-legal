2. Who can use Dafa

  • You must be 16 or over. We check your date of birth when you sign up, and it must be your real one.
  • An account is for one person. Do not share it, and do not create an account for someone else.
  • You may use a nickname. Your real name is optional.

3. What Dafa is, and what it is not

Dafa connects you with mental-health specialists for private conversations, and gives you tools to look after your day: a journal, homework your specialist sets you, and community sessions.

  • Dafa does not diagnose you. The app makes no automated assessment, score or diagnosis. Anything clinical is the judgement of the specialist you are talking to.
  • Dafa is not an emergency service and is not a substitute for in-person or emergency care.
  • Calls are arranged between you and your specialist outside the app and are logged in it. Dafa does not place live calls itself.

4. Specialists

Specialists on Dafa are independent professionals. Each one is invited, reviewed and approved by the Dafa team before they appear in the app, and can be removed if they stop meeting our standards.

Specialists are not employed by Dafa. Each specialist is responsible for the professional advice and care they give you.

5. Your account

  • Keep your password private. We may also send a sign-in code to your email to confirm it is you.
  • Tell us straight away at dafa@getkensho.ai if you think someone else has used your account.
  • Keep the details you give us accurate, especially your date of birth and any emergency contact.

6. Acceptable use

Dafa is a place for support. In chats, reviews, complaints and community sessions, you must not:

  • harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against anyone;
  • share sexual content, or content that exploits or endangers anyone;
  • pretend to be someone else, or misrepresent your qualifications;
  • share another person's private information, or record and share a session or conversation without everyone's consent;
  • use Dafa to advertise, sell, or move a paid relationship outside the app to avoid its payments;
  • post reviews that are false, or that are not about your own experience;
  • try to break, overload, scrape or get around the app's security or its limits.

Specialists are also bound by their own professional obligations.

7. Reporting and moderation

  • Report a specialist: open the chat menu and choose Report this doctor. You can attach the message concerned.
  • Anything else, including a review you think breaks these terms: go to Profile → Complaints & reviews → Write a complaint or review, or write to dafa@getkensho.ai.
  • Specialists can also raise a complaint about a member.

Our administrators review reports. They work with anonymous user IDs and see a message only when it is attached to a report. Depending on what happened, they may:

  • give a warning;
  • suspend an account for a time, or permanently;
  • cancel a subscription;
  • remove the text of a review.

We may also act without a report when we must, to keep someone safe or to meet the law.

8. Your messages and content

  • What you write and upload stays yours. You let us store it and show it to the people you share it with, only so the app can work.
  • You can delete a message for both sides within 30 minutes of sending it.
  • Your chats are encrypted. Privacy comes first. This is not end-to-end encryption; the Privacy Policy explains exactly what it means.
  • Reviews you post are public, under your display name or nickname.

9. Payments

  • No automatic renewal: a subscription does not renew on its own. When it ends, you choose whether to renew.

Refunds
